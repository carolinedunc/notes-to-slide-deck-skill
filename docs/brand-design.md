# Duncan & Co.

A fictional company designed to exhibit brand-specific slide deck formatting

## Wordmark
"DUNCAN & CO." — navy, bold, small caps, positioned top-left of every slide.

## Logo mark
Logo designed in Canva and exported as a .png file. 
Imported via function in 'python-pptx'. 
Positioned top left next to wordmark.

## Colors
| Role | Hex Code |
|---|---|
| Primary (navy) | `#1B3A5C` |
| Accent (orange) | `#E8792C` |
| Secondary accent (yellow/gold) | `#F2B233` |
| Background | `#FAFAFA` |
| Body text (charcoal) | `#2B2B2B` |

## Header
Wordmark + logo mark top-left. Slide title bold and large beneath it. 
Thin orange horizontal rule under the title.

## Footer
"Duncan & Co." in small gray text, bottom-left. Slide number, bottom-right. 
Thin navy rule above the footer text.
